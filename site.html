GOOD TO KNOW

A few things
you might be wondering.

We’re building ErrandX with clear steps and no surprises.

Where is ErrandX available?+

We’re starting in selected service areas across Kano State. Choose your neighbourhood in the availability checker above to see the current demo coverage.

When do I pay for a food order?+

The restaurant accepts your order before checkout. The platform confirms payment before pickup credentials are issued.

How do you confirm pickup and delivery?+

ErrandX is designed for order-specific pickup and recipient verification. This website preview does not collect verification codes or make deliveries.

Can my business join ErrandX?+

Restaurants, small-chops vendors, individual riders and fleet companies can apply to join the marketplace when onboarding opens in their service area.
